QuickBooks

QuickBooks is accounting software that helps small and medium-sized businesses manage finances by automating tasks like bookkeeping, invoicing, and payroll. It tracks income and expenses, generates financial reports, and provides tools for managing vendors and customers. Its features allow businesses to streamline financial management, monitor cash flow, and simplify tax preparation.
